# François Dequenne — EcoVadis Interim Manager

## In brief

François Dequenne supports Belgian SMEs through their EcoVadis rating as **EcoVadis Interim Manager** at ESG Interim Management ([esgim.eu](https://esgim.eu)). His background as a company director — not a consultant — is what makes the support practical rather than theoretical.

## Bio

An engineer by training, François built an international career across several technical sectors and in very different configurations: growth, restructuring, post-acquisition, in Belgium and abroad. He notably ran subsidiaries for fifteen years, with full responsibility for P&L and teams.

That experience as a boss is what makes EcoVadis support useful rather than theoretical. A managing director knows what a company can actually commit to: the balance between long-term objectives and the budgeted actions that can start this quarter without putting operations under strain.

General management also gives a 360° view of the company — precisely what an EcoVadis assessment demands. Environment maps to production and processes, the home ground of his engineering training. Sustainable Procurement is about commerce and supplier relationships, the core of his career. The social pillar runs through human resources, ethics through governance, data security through IT. None of these fields is foreign to him — and above all, none seems more important than the others.

He therefore works as an interim manager: he takes charge of the journey end to end, speaks each department's language, and leaves behind an organisation able to hold its score without him.
